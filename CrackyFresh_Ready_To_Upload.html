Kitchen inspiration

Simple ways to enjoy CrackyFresh

Easy ideas to help customers get more from their ingredients.

🥞
EGG POWDER

Fluffy breakfast pancakes

Prepare egg powder according to the verified pack directions. Combine with flour, milk, baking powder and a little oil. Cook small pancakes until golden.

🥤
SPINACH POWDER

Green banana smoothie

Blend banana, plain yoghurt, milk and a small amount of spinach powder. Adjust to taste and serve chilled.

🍝
BEETROOT POWDER

Rosy pasta dough

Mix a little beetroot powder into flour before adding eggs and water to make colourful homemade pasta.

🍳
LIQUID EGGS

Family-size omelette

Thaw refrigerated frozen eggs safely according to validated instructions. Cook thoroughly with peppers, onions and spinach.

🥣
DRIED BANANA & PAPAYA

Tropical breakfast bowl

Top oats or yoghurt with chopped dried banana, dried papaya and toasted seeds for texture and flavour.

🍪
DRIED FRUIT

Fruit & oat bites

Combine finely chopped dried fruit with oats and nut butter. Shape into bites and chill before serving.

Papaya seed powder and iboza (ginger bush) powder are not included in consumption recipes pending botanical identity, food-safety and regulatory review.
